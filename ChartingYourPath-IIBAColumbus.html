#### Future State of the Business Analyst Hero ---
Format:1-2-4-All
Objective:participants first think individually, then discuss in pairs, followed by small groups of four, and finally share insights with the entire group.
Goal:A shared understanding of the Business Analyst behavior beyond today
Time:22m
Thought: "What are the key responsibilities and skills that should define the future job description of a business analyst?"
1m: individual
2m: pair pick some common themes
4m: group of 4 aim to identify 2-3 common themes or ideas that resonate with everyone in the group.
All 15m: Pick a group note taker or flip chart. Each group of four to shares their identified themes or ideas.
